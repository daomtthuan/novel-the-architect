# Artifact Registry (v6, World Calendar canon)

## The Ark
Function: catastrophic defense system and later space-time observation system.
Symbolism: love, sacrifice, technology, consequence.
States: early weapon system; consciousness core (Elena becomes this at WC35); observation system (Movie 2); timeline interface (Movie 3).

## Eterna Ring / Eterna Bond
Elena and the Architect's wedding rings (WC24). Now formally established as **Eterna-make heirloom pieces**, tied to Elena's people (see `05-world.md`) rather than a purely poetic name — this resolves what was previously just a naming coincidence in the Visual Bible.

## Architect's Notebooks
Contain inventions, observations, personal thoughts, unresolved problems — including, in the earliest volumes, half-formed notes from childhood that already show adult-level reasoning. Important because technical notes may contain emotional subtext.

## Elena's Personal Objects
Objects retained by family — jewelry, letters, clothing, magical images, household objects. Use sparingly as emotional anchors, especially in Iris's and Seraphine's later scenes with the Son.

## The Teacher's Relic
A personal item of The Teacher's, kept by the Architect after WC16 — new artifact, first mention. Functions as a private reminder of his first great failure. Not to be confused with any Elena-associated object.

## Observatory Artifacts
Ancient objects associated with timeline observation and Builder detection.

## Artifact Rule

Do not introduce an artifact as a plot-solving device without establishing origin, function, limitations, cost, previous mentions.
