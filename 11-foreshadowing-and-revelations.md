# Foreshadowing and Revelation Matrix (v6, World Calendar canon)

## Season 1 — Reader Knowledge

Allowed: the infant-body/adult-mind reincarnation premise (established early); magic; The Teacher and his death; Elena; Iris; political powers; Ark danger; Seraphine's arrival and defection.

Do not openly reveal: Observatory truth; Quantum Anchor; Builder mechanics; the "Architect vs. Builder" distinction as an explicit rule; full multiverse mechanics; The Other Builder; Original Timeline.

## Season 1 Clues

Use: ancient records mentioning previous transmigrated figures (future Other Builders lore); impossible similarities between magical systems; Ark anomalies; Elena's strange dreams/resonance; the protagonist's occasional déjà vu; old structures using familiar principles; the mentor's own half-finished theories about "others like you."

## Movie 1

Reveal: previous otherworlder/Builder records (still not fully explained); hidden Ark records; historical inconsistencies; Observatory references; the full truth of Elena's sacrifice (to the Son, WC75).

Keep the full Builder/Architect distinction hidden as an explicit rule — Movie 1 shows the emotional result (his refusal to explain himself) without naming the mechanism.

## Movie 2

Reveal: timeline branches; Observatory; the Quantum Anchor; the term "Builder" and its basic meaning; Reality Convergence begins; Aion.

## Movie 3

Reveal: The Other Builder's full timeline; the Architect/Builder distinction as explicit rule; alternate Elenas; consequences of saving Elena by force; absence of a perfect timeline.

## Movie 4

Reveal: Original Timeline; Earth connection; an ordinary alternate Builder; final meaning of freedom.

## Rule

Good foreshadowing works twice. First reading: strange detail. Second reading: obvious clue. Do not use spoilers disguised as mysterious narration.
