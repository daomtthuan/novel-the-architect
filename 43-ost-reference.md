# OST Reference (v6, World Calendar canon)

## Purpose

This file maps the project's reference soundtrack to specific scenes, WC years, and emotional beats.

These tracks are **mood/tone references only** — they inform pacing, atmosphere, and emotional restraint when drafting a scene. They are not canon and should never be described, quoted, or referenced inside the prose itself.

---

## Track Index

### 01 — The Architect's Theme: "The Architect"
**Function:** Core character theme.
**Use for:** establishing the protagonist's internal voice (from early childhood, WC0+); moments of systems-thinking, competence, quiet resolve; recurring motif reminding the reader who is at the center of the story.
**Important:** "The Architect" is a title earned at WC35, not the protagonist's personal name, and not synonymous with "Builder."

### 02 — The Architect's Theme: "A Place to Stay"
**Function:** Softer variation — belonging rather than competence.
**Use for:** WC15–24 (Elena and the road out of the Darkest Point); domestic scenes, found-family warmth; the shift from surviving in this world to wanting to stay in it.

### 03 — Elena's Theme: "Her Quiet Light"
**Function:** Elena's character theme.
**Use for:** scenes establishing Elena as a full person; her role breaking the Darkest Point (WC23); warmth, humor, perceptiveness; trust and vulnerability; proposal/wedding; quiet domestic scenes; Mira-era rediscovery of Elena through records.
Elena's theme should primarily represent Elena alive, not merely her death.

### 03B — Iris's Theme: "What I Didn't Say" *(NEW)*
**Function:** Iris's character theme.
**Use for:** her quiet, adult-only attraction (~WC21+); her withdrawal at the wedding (WC24); her sword-training scenes with the Son. Should never underscore any scene before she is textually an adult.

### 03C — Seraphine's Theme: "Borrowed Warmth" *(NEW)*
**Function:** Seraphine's character theme.
**Use for:** her provocation era (WC33, played mischievously); her defection (WC35, shifting tone); her guilt-driven visits and refusals (post-WC35, warmer/sadder variant).

### 04 — The Ark: "The Ark Awakens"
**Function:** Concept/artifact theme.
**Use for:** Ark Stage 1→2 transition; first anomalies; Movie 2 Observatory-era revelations; Mira's first impossible record involving the Ark.

### 05 — The Ark: "Activation"
**Function:** Climax variant — Great War payoff.
**Use for:** activation sequence (WC35); large-scale, irreversible-decision pacing; Great War climax.
**Caution:** Reserve this track's intensity for its designated climax.

### 06 — Ark Countdown: "Her Choice, My Silence"
**Function:** Emotional core of Elena's sacrifice.
**Use for:** moments immediately before/during Elena choosing to become the Ark's core (WC35); the Architect's inability to stop her; silence rather than speech.
**Restriction:** SINGLE-USE / RESERVED for Elena's choice. Do not reuse for generic Elena sadness, flashbacks, alternate Elenas, or ordinary grief.

### 07 — "Empty House"
**Function:** Grief/aftermath theme.
**Use for:** the Architect returning home alone after Elena's death; recurring empty-house motif; Movie 1 flashbacks to the son visiting the old home; later callbacks.

### 08 — "Home"
**Function:** Resolution/legacy theme — the answer to "Empty House."
**Use for:** final Home sequence (Movie 4); Noah choosing freedom; moments where home completes its symbolic evolution: physical shelter → Elena → family → city → civilization → freedom.
This track is the emotional bookend to Track 07.

### 09 — The Last Observer: "The Truth"
**Function:** Movie 2 revelation theme.
**Use for:** Mira's investigative breakthroughs; Observatory encounters; Quantum Anchor confirmation; first explicit "Builder" reveal; Reality Convergence beginning.

### 10 — "Two Builders" *(NEW)*
**Function:** Movie 3 confrontation theme.
**Use for:** scenes directly contrasting the Architect and The Other Builder; the moment The Other Builder's choice (saving his Elena by force) is fully explained.
**Caution:** reserve for direct confrontation/contrast scenes; do not use as generic Movie 3 background music.

---

## Usage Guidelines

- OST references are for the writing agent's internal tone calibration.
- Never mention track titles, music, or soundtrack metadata inside normal chapter prose.
- Reserve high-intensity tracks (05, 06, 10) for their designated scenes.
- Treat Tracks 07 and 08 as a matched pair across the full saga.
- Consult this file alongside `19-emotional-memory.md` and `20-scene-memory.md`.
- OST supports emotion; it does not replace emotional writing.

## Update Rule

If new OST tracks are added, record: track title; function; suggested scene(s)/WC year; reservation/caution; relationship to existing motifs. Do not assign a single track to too many unrelated scenes.
