# Organization Registry (v9, World Calendar canon)

## The Architect's Administration

Function: infrastructure, governance, public services.

## Mage Academy

Function: research, education, magical standardization. The Teacher belonged to this world before his death (WC16).

## Major Churches

Multiple denominations may exist. Avoid one-dimensional religious villains.

## Royal Governments

Political entities affected by Architect technology — multiple Kingdoms, none of them a single dominant hegemon (see `06-factions-and-politics.md`).

## Empires

### Empire of Veyrand

Home polity of Isolde Veyrand. Sponsors her WC26 political marriage proposal to the Architect (refused). Becomes a political ally, then a critical wartime ally from WC32.

## The Guild (updated v9)

Function: professional adventurer's guild; original professional home of the five-person A-rank party that included the Architect's Father, Mother, The Teacher (mage), the Guild Master (tanker), and the Scout/Investigator, before and after their retirement/deaths.
Leadership: from WC16 onward, led by the surviving tanker member as **Guild Master** — the sole adult survivor of the original five.
Function post-WC16: becomes a durable ally and resource base for the Architect through his rise, offering material and political support.
**Function post-WC17 (new, v9):** as of Chapter 13, the Architect is formally registered as an **independent adventurer** through the Guild — taking low-tier solo missions (reconnaissance, minor Demon clearing near the settlement) specifically to build real combat experience without depending on a party. The Guild Master accepts this registration without pressuring him to reconsider, but privately holds unresolved concern about the Architect's insistence on fighting entirely alone. This registration is a durable, ongoing status, not a one-time event — the Architect continues taking missions through the Guild in subsequent chapters unless narratively changed.

## Demon Factions

Multiple internal factions. Seraphine's original faction is the one whose true domination-seeking agenda triggers her defection (WC34–35).

**The high-tier Demon encountered at WC16** — continuity name "The Unburied One" — is critically wounded rather than killed at WC16, spends WC16–32 dormant while secretly influencing Seraphine's faction from the shadows. Presumed (not confirmed) destroyed at WC40. **New (v9, Chapter 13):** the Architect, through his independent Guild missions, has begun compiling fragmentary, HINTED-level rumors of unusual Demon activity from other adventurers — these rumors are not yet connected to any specific faction or individual, and must not be explicitly linked to The Unburied One in prose during Season 1.

## The Observatory

Ancient organization studying reality and, centrally, the Builder phenomenon. Hierarchy includes Observers and Aion.

## Returnists

Movie 4 faction seeking restoration of the Original Timeline.

## Organization Rule

Every recurring organization must have: goal, leadership, resources, ideology, allies, enemies, internal conflict, historical evolution.
