# Organization Registry (v7, World Calendar canon)

## The Architect's Administration
Function: infrastructure, governance, public services.

## Mage Academy
Function: research, education, magical standardization. The Teacher belonged to this world before his death (WC16).

## Major Churches
Multiple denominations may exist. Avoid one-dimensional religious villains.

## Royal Governments
Political entities affected by Architect technology — multiple Kingdoms, none of them a single dominant hegemon (see `06-factions-and-politics.md`).

## Empires

### Empire of Veyrand
Home polity of Isolde Veyrand. Sponsors her WC26 political marriage proposal to the Architect (refused). Becomes a political ally, then a critical wartime ally from WC32. **Corrected v7:** previously miscategorized as "Isolde's Kingdom" in this file — this was a naming conflict resolved by `45-session-update-wc16-revision-and-worldbuilding.md` §1; Veyrand is an Empire.

## The Guild (new, added v7 per session update 45 §6)
Function: professional adventurer's guild; original professional home of the five-person A-rank party that included the Architect's Father, Mother, The Teacher (mage), the Guild Master (tanker), and the Scout/Investigator, before and after their retirement/deaths.
Leadership: from WC16 onward, led by the surviving tanker member as **Guild Master** — the sole adult survivor of the original five (he was absent, coordinating the guild's response, during the WC16 battle).
Function post-WC16: becomes a durable ally and resource base for the Architect through his rise, offering material and political support.

## Demon Factions
Multiple internal factions. Seraphine's original faction is the one whose true domination-seeking agenda triggers her defection (WC34–35). The high-tier Demon encountered at WC16 is a separate, earlier thread — see `28-unresolved-threads.md` (fate OPEN).

## The Observatory
Ancient organization studying reality and, centrally, the Builder phenomenon. Hierarchy includes Observers and Aion.

## Returnists
Movie 4 faction seeking restoration of the Original Timeline.

## Organization Rule

Every recurring organization must have: goal, leadership, resources, ideology, allies, enemies, internal conflict, historical evolution.
