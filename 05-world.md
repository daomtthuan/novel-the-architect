# World Bible (v6, World Calendar canon)

## World Identity

A fantasy civilization with kingdoms, empires, city-states, villages, magical academies, churches, monster territories, demon civilizations, ancient ruins, magical infrastructure.

The world should initially feel like fantasy, not disguised science fiction.

## Peoples

### Human
Standard 1.0× aging rate. The Architect and Iris are Human.

### Eterna
A long-lived, Light/Time-affinity people. Age at 0.25× the Human rate. Elena is Eterna. Their naming lineage explains existing artifact names — "Eterna Ring," "Eterna Bond" (wedding rings, see `24-artifact-registry.md` / `30-visual-bible.md`) — as Eterna-make heirloom pieces.

### Half-blood (Human × Eterna)
Age at 0.5× the Human rate. The Son is the setting's clearest example.

### Demon / Succubus
Varies by subtype; Succubi such as Seraphine are effectively ageless.

## Civilization Stages

### Early
Magic is personal and localized.

### Architect Era (roughly Era 1–7, WC 0–82)
Magic becomes infrastructure: communication, transportation, energy, agriculture, medicine, defense.

### Post-Architect Era (Era 8, WC 83–91 onward)
Magic and science merge.

### Mira Era (Movie 2–3, WC ≈182–192)
Civilization can manipulate space-time and observe timeline branches; the truth of the Builders becomes accessible to a rare few.

## The Architect's City

Growth comes from reliable infrastructure, safe trade, accessible magic, improved food production, migration, stable institutions.

## Home

Symbolic evolution:

physical shelter → Elena → family → city → civilization → freedom

## Historical Memory

Series (Season 1): The Architect is alive.

Movie 1: he becomes, then dies as, a deeply contested/understood historical figure to his own son.

Movie 2: legend, ~100 years gone.

Movie 3: legend under active historical revision, ~110 years gone.

Movie 4: nearly forgotten, ~1,000 years gone.

The erosion of historical memory is intentional.
