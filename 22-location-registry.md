# Location Registry (v7, World Calendar canon)

## Architect's Original Home
Function: private home shared with Elena, later with the Son, Iris, and Seraphine's frequent presence.
Symbolism: home, family, loss.
Continuity: must remain visually consistent.

## Architect's City — renamed "Elena" (updated v7)
Function: first major civilization built under Architect influence, begun WC28, on the site of the Architect's own birthplace/childhood home — where he also first met Elena (WC15).
History: pre-rename, the city's leader was a close personal friend of the Architect's father, which is why the Architect's early inventions were permitted for civic use ahead of formal recognition. Heavily destroyed during the Great War (WC35). Rebuilt by the Architect after the war; he personally renames it **"Elena"** after her death.
Political form: independent city-state, not a monarchy — the Architect never claims rulership.
Features: magical lights, transportation, infrastructure, public institutions.
Symbolism: human-scale civilization; the "Home" motif's central physical anchor.
**OPEN:** exact stylization of the final name — see `05-world.md`.
