# Location Registry (v6, World Calendar canon)

## Architect's Original Home
Function: private home shared with Elena, later with the Son, Iris, and Seraphine's frequent presence.
Symbolism: home, family, loss.
Continuity: must remain visually consistent.

## Architect's City
Function: first major civilization built under Architect influence, begun WC28.
Features: magical lights, transportation, infrastructure, public institutions.
Symbolism: human-scale civilization.

## The Teacher's Old Study / Training Ground
Function: site of the Architect's early magical education (WC10–16); later a quiet, rarely-visited memorial space.

## Capital
Political center.

## Magic Academy
Research and education center; expands significantly during The Reform (~WC48–52).

## Great War Battlefield
Major historical location; includes the site of The Teacher's death (WC16) and the Ark activation site (WC35) — these should be treated as geographically or thematically related where useful.

## Ark Chamber
Location of Elena's final choice (WC35).

## Observatory
Ancient / high-level research location; central to Movie 2–3.

## Mira's Archive
Important Movie 2 investigation location.

## Original Timeline Earth
Movie 4 location.

## Location Rule

When adding a new recurring location, record: name, geography, function, political ownership, visual identity, first appearance, later appearances.
