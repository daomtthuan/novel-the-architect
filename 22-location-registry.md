# Location Registry (v8, World Calendar canon)

## Architect's Original Home
Function: private home shared with Elena, later with the Son, Iris, and Seraphine's frequent presence.
Symbolism: home, family, loss.
Continuity: must remain visually consistent.

## Architect's City — renamed "Elena" (RESOLVED, v8)
Function: first major civilization built under Architect influence, begun WC28, on the site of the Architect's own birthplace/childhood home — where he also first met Elena (WC15).
History: pre-rename, the city's leader was a close personal friend of the Architect's father, which is why the Architect's early inventions were permitted for civic use ahead of formal recognition. As the city's reputation grows (~WC28–31), informal use begins calling it "the Architect's City" — a nickname he privately dislikes and never adopts (see `05-world.md`). Heavily destroyed during the Great War (WC35). Rebuilt by the Architect after the war; he personally renames it **"Elena"** after her death — both a memorial and a final, deliberate refusal of the "Architect's City" framing.
Political form: independent city-state, not a monarchy — the Architect never claims rulership.
Features: magical lights, transportation, infrastructure, public institutions.
Symbolism: human-scale civilization; the "Home" motif's central physical anchor.
**Final stylization: plain "Elena."** RESOLVED, v8 — previously OPEN. Do not reintroduce an alternate stylization without explicit owner instruction.
