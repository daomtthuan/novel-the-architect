# OST Reference

## Purpose

This file maps the project's reference soundtrack to specific scenes, chapters, and emotional beats.

These tracks are **mood/tone references only** — they inform pacing, atmosphere, and emotional restraint when drafting a scene. They are not canon and should never be described, quoted, or referenced inside the prose itself.

---

## Track Index

### 01 — The Architect's Theme: "The Architect"

**Function:** Core character theme.

**Use for:**
- establishing the protagonist's internal voice;
- moments of systems-thinking, competence, quiet resolve;
- recurring motif whenever the narrative wants to remind the reader who is at the center of the story.

**Important:** "The Architect" is a title / epithet, not the protagonist's personal name.

### 02 — The Architect's Theme: "A Place to Stay"

**Function:** Softer variation of the character theme — belonging rather than competence.

**Use for:**
- Era 2–3 (Elena and Discovery / Nation Building);
- domestic scenes, found-family warmth;
- the shift from surviving in this world to wanting to stay in this world.

### 03 — Elena's Theme: "Her Quiet Light"

**Function:** Elena's character theme.

**Use for:**
- scenes establishing Elena as a full person, not a plot device;
- warmth, humor, perceptiveness;
- trust and vulnerability;
- proposal / wedding;
- quiet domestic scenes;
- Mira-era rediscovery of Elena through records.

Elena's theme should primarily represent Elena alive, not merely her death.

### 04 — The Ark: "The Ark Awakens"

**Function:** Concept / artifact theme — first emergence of the Ark as something beyond a weapon.

**Use for:**
- Ark Stage 1→2 transition;
- first anomalies;
- Season 3 Observatory-era revelations;
- Mira's first impossible record involving the Ark.

### 05 — The Ark: "Activation"

**Function:** Climax variant — Great War payoff.

**Use for:**
- activation sequence;
- large-scale, high-stakes, irreversible-decision pacing;
- Great War climax.

**Caution:** Reserve this track's intensity for its designated climax. Do not reuse it for smaller stakes.

### 06 — Ark Countdown: "Her Choice, My Silence"

**Function:** Emotional core of Elena's sacrifice.

**Use for:**
- moments immediately before / during Elena choosing to become the Ark's core;
- The Architect's inability to stop her;
- silence rather than speech.

**Restriction:** SINGLE-USE / RESERVED for Elena's choice.

Do not reuse it for generic Elena sadness, flashbacks, alternate Elenas, or ordinary grief.

### 07 — "Empty House"

**Function:** Grief / aftermath theme.

**Use for:**
- The Architect returning home alone after Elena's death;
- recurring empty-house motif;
- S2 son visiting the old home;
- later callbacks to the original home as a symbol of loss.

### 08 — "Home"

**Function:** Resolution / legacy theme — the answer to "Empty House."

**Use for:**
- final Home sequence;
- Noah choosing freedom;
- moments where home completes its symbolic evolution:
  physical shelter → Elena → family → city → civilization → freedom.

This track is the emotional bookend to Track 07.

### 09 — The Last Observer: "The Truth"

**Function:** Season 3 revelation theme.

**Use for:**
- Mira's investigative breakthroughs;
- Observatory encounters;
- Quantum Anchor confirmation;
- Reality Convergence beginning;
- major Season 3 mystery-board reveals.

---

## Usage Guidelines

- OST references are for the writing agent's internal tone calibration.
- Never mention track titles, music, or soundtrack metadata inside normal chapter prose.
- Reserve high-intensity tracks (05, 06) for their designated scenes.
- Treat Tracks 07 and 08 as a matched pair across the five-season arc.
- Consult this file alongside `19-emotional-memory.md` and `20-scene-memory.md`.
- OST supports emotion; it does not replace emotional writing.

## Update Rule

If new OST tracks are added, record:
- track title;
- function;
- suggested scene(s) or season;
- reservation / caution;
- relationship to existing motifs.

Do not assign a single track to too many unrelated scenes.
