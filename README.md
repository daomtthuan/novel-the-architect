## Important

`INSTRUCTIONS.md` is the orchestrator.

`29-agent-memory.md` is mutable short-term state.

Detailed canon and registries remain authoritative over agent memory. `world_calendar.md` is authoritative over all of them for dates and ages.

The protagonist has no personal name. "The Architect" is a title, earned WC35, distinct from the broader category "Builder."

Season 1 is written in first person, from birth, for reader embodiment.

Elena remains the primary heroine. Iris, Seraphine, and Isolde are the three secondary heroines; none of them ever displaces or replaces Elena.

Romance and fan-service are secondary narrative layers and must never override the main plot or emotional continuity — and must never place romantic framing on a character before adulthood.

OST is internal tone calibration and must not be exposed as metadata in prose.
