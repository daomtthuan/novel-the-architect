### WC19 — The Full Outing, the Anh/Em Shift, and "A Place to Stay" (new, Chapter 16)

- **Event ID:** ROM-S01-C016-01
- **WC Year:** 19
- **Characters:** Architect, Elena
- **Character Ages (computed):** Architect 19; Elena 24.75
- **Relationship State Before:** RECONCILIATION (in progress) — conditional forgiveness granted, outing pending (per ROM-S01-C015-01).
- **Interaction:** The Architect and Elena spend a full day together entirely free of research or problem-solving, exactly as Elena required. Elena proposes that they stop using "chị/con" and switch to "anh/em," explicitly citing that the Architect is now an adult and that the old address no longer fits. They haggle badly (his side) at the market, eat at the same roadside food stall from Chapter 8, and walk up a hillside overlooking the valley — a place Elena used to visit alone before the Architect was even born. Watching the sunset and then a full night sky, the Architect spontaneously says this world's stars are more beautiful than Earth's, and that for the first time he wants to protect something — the sky, the village, the people he loves — purely because he wants to, not out of fear. He voices, unprompted, a wish to build "a place to stay." Elena does not promise outright, but says she would want to be there if such a place were ever built. The day ends with a light kiss on the cheek.
- **Relationship State After:** RECONCILIATION, meaningfully deepened but explicitly not complete. Elena is careful to frame the day as provisional ("hôm nay chỉ là một ngày; anh còn rất nhiều ngày khác để chứng minh").
- **Emotional Meaning:** The first sustained, on-page instance of the Architect wanting something purely for its own sake rather than for utility or control — a direct emotional precursor to, but explicitly distinct from, the still-pending WC23 breakthrough. The anh/em shift marks the relationship's move onto genuinely adult, mutual footing.
- **Joke / Running Motif:** The Architect's poor haggling skills (callback to Chapter 8's "you always pay too much" joke) — Elena teases that Iris would be disappointed in him.
- **Foreshadowing:** "A place to stay" is now a direct, on-page seed for the City Project (WC28) and the eventual renaming of the city to "Elena" post-WC35 — see `05-world.md`, `22-location-registry.md`. Direct callback to Elena's Chapter 8 "temporary name" line ("chị hy vọng đó là một cái tên anh lớn lên để xứng đáng với nó").
- **Reader Interpretation:** Warmth and genuine hope, tempered by dramatic irony — the reader (unlike the Architect) may sense this hillside wish is the true origin of the city that will one day bear Elena's name.
- **Character Interpretation:** Neither character treats this day as erasing the WC18 rupture or as a substitute for the deeper ideological change Elena still hopes for; Elena's non-promise ("chị nghĩ chị muốn ở đó, nếu anh thực sự xây được nó") is sincere but deliberately conditional.
- **Must Be Remembered Later:** The anh/em address must be preserved consistently in all future chapters unless a deliberate regression scene is written. "A place to stay" must eventually pay off directly at the City Project (WC28) and again in the city's renaming after WC35 — it should not be over-referenced in the meantime. The WC23 "People are not variables" breakthrough remains the next required major Architect ↔ Elena milestone and must not be skipped because of this chapter's warmth.
