## Recurring Motifs

Track: private jokes; nicknames or titles that are actually permitted; favorite foods; shared places; embarrassing incidents; promises; gifts; jealousy triggers; meaningful gestures.

Seed entries to track from the outset:

- Elena's jealousy of Seraphine (WC33) — must be referenced again, at least once, after Elena's death (e.g., Seraphine acknowledging it to the grown Son).
- Iris's self-withdrawal at the wedding (WC24) — must never be silently reversed into a later confession.
- Isolde's "relief, not offense" at being refused — must be referenced again when Elena consents to a second marriage and the Architect still refuses.
- Seraphine's request to be called "Sera" (post-WC35) — a granted concession (a name) paired with a refusal (everything else); do not let the nickname read as the Architect softening toward romance.
- Seraphine's genuine, unspoken love and the resulting loss of her demonic combat power — the causal link (love → power loss) should stay implicit/dramatic-irony for most of the story; do not have her state it outright to the Architect unless a specific chapter is built around that reveal.

## Rule

A romantic interaction that becomes important must be remembered. The AI agent should not make a character behave as though an emotionally significant previous interaction never happened.

## Elena Memory

Especially track: first meeting (WC15); the WC18 major argument during the Architect's Darkest Point; the WC19 reconciliation; the WC23 ideological breakthrough; first confession; proposal; wedding (WC24); first home; family moments; major disagreements; final sacrifice (WC35); objects/phrases associated with her.

### WC18 — Major Argument

- **WC Year:** 18
- **Characters:** Architect, Elena
- **Relationship State Before:** Elena is still actively trying to keep the Architect from becoming consumed by his increasingly optimization-driven worldview.
- **Interaction:** The Architect's first genuinely successful Magic research reinforces his tendency to view problems through optimization. Elena tries to pull him away from his growing darkness, but fails. They have a major argument.
- **Relationship State After:** Emotional rupture; they remain connected, but the conflict is unresolved in the moment.
- **Emotional Meaning:** An important early fracture showing that Elena's role is not merely to comfort the Architect; she actively challenges the direction he is taking.
- **Must Be Remembered Later:** Do not write later scenes as though their relationship had no serious conflict before WC23. The WC18 argument is an established part of their shared history.

### WC19 — Reconciliation

- **WC Year:** 19
- **Characters:** Architect, Elena
- **Relationship State Before:** They are carrying the unresolved emotional weight of the WC18 argument.
- **Interaction:** After the period of conflict, the Architect and Elena reconcile while the Architect's Magic technology begins to emerge and his influence among Mages grows.
- **Relationship State After:** Their relationship is restored, without erasing the disagreement that preceded it.
- **Emotional Meaning:** Their bond survives a serious ideological and emotional conflict. Reconciliation is part of their relationship continuity, not a reset that makes the WC18 argument irrelevant.
- **Must Be Remembered Later:** Later chapters may show further disagreements, but must not imply that WC18 permanently separated them or that WC18 never happened.

## Iris / Seraphine / Isolde Memory

Track each woman's key unresolved beat separately, and make sure none of the three is allowed to quietly "win" the Architect's heart in a later installment — their arcs resolve as devotion-without-requital (Iris, Seraphine) or devotion-without-marriage (Isolde), not as delayed romantic victories.
