Guild Master survives, becomes a durable political/material ally through The Guild from WC16 on. *(This event is now fully dramatized on-page in Chapters 11–13 — treat those chapters, not this archive, as the primary source for scene-level detail.)*

## B. City name & WC16 Demon fate resolution (was `47`)
