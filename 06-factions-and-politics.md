# Factions and Political Forces (v6, World Calendar canon)

## Political Principle

The Architect becomes politically important because his civilization changes the balance of power, even though he does not initially seek political authority. His rise runs alongside his personal Darkest Point era (WC16–23) — his early political entanglements are colored by the same "people as variables" thinking that costs him The Teacher.

## Kingdoms

Interests: territory, nobles, security, trade, magical resources. Not inherently evil. One Kingdom sponsors Isolde's WC26 marriage proposal.

## Empires

Interests: strategic technology, resources, magical infrastructure, geopolitical dominance.

## Churches

Possible positions: support modernization; distrust otherworlders/Builders; worship divine powers; fear magic; seek political control. Do not make every religious organization evil.

## Mage Institutions

Seek knowledge, magical technology, research, access to the Architect's systems. The Teacher belonged to this world before his death at WC16.

## Demon Civilization

Not every demon is evil. Includes internal factions, political motives, cultural differences, competing ideologies. Seraphine originates from a faction whose leadership (as she discovers WC33–34) actually seeks domination rather than mere survival — this is what triggers her defection.

## The Observatory

Ancient organization studying timelines, consciousness, magic, reality branches, and — centrally — **Builders**: transmigrated/reincarnated consciousnesses with systems-shaping aptitude.

Core belief:

> Uncontrolled Builders can destabilize reality.

## Aion

Represents stability through control.

## Returnists

Movie 4 faction.

Belief:

> One world, one history, one truth.

Goal: collapse branches and restore the Original Timeline. They are ideologically dangerous because their argument is understandable.
