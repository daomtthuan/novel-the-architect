# Factions and Political Forces (v8, World Calendar canon)

## Political Baseline (added v7, per session update 45 §1)

The world contains many nations — multiple Empires alongside multiple Kingdoms — with no single dominant hegemon. Power is balanced; active diplomacy, rivalry, and war exist between polities on an ongoing basis. The **Empire of Veyrand** is one such Empire (see below), not the world's sole power center.

## Political Principle

The Architect becomes politically important because his civilization changes the balance of power, even though he does not initially seek political authority. His rise runs alongside his personal Darkest Point era (WC16–23) — his early political entanglements are colored by the same "people as variables" thinking that costs him his parents, The Teacher, and the Scout at WC16.

## Kingdoms

Interests: territory, nobles, security, trade, magical resources. Not inherently evil.

## Empires

Interests: strategic technology, resources, magical infrastructure, geopolitical dominance.

### Empire of Veyrand

A confirmed Empire (not a Kingdom — corrected v7; see `45-session-update-wc16-revision-and-worldbuilding.md`). Home polity of Isolde Veyrand. **The Empire of Veyrand sponsors Isolde's WC26 marriage proposal** to the Architect. Refused; becomes a durable political ally, then a critical wartime ally from WC32.

## Churches

Possible positions: support modernization; distrust otherworlders/Builders; worship divine powers; fear magic; seek political control. Do not make every religious organization evil.

## Mage Institutions

Seek knowledge, magical technology, research, access to the Architect's systems. The Teacher belonged to this world before his death at WC16.

## Demon Civilization

Not every demon is evil. Includes internal factions, political motives, cultural differences, competing ideologies. Seraphine originates from a faction whose leadership (as she discovers WC33–34) actually seeks domination rather than mere survival — this is what triggers her defection.

**The Unburied One (new, v8).** The high-tier Demon encountered at WC16 (`28-unresolved-threads.md`) survives, critically wounded, and spends WC16–32 dormant. During dormancy it secretly and indirectly influences Seraphine's domination-seeking faction from the shadows — a hidden patron, not a commander, and unknown to both the faction's own leadership and to Seraphine. Presumed (not confirmed) destroyed at WC40. This connection should remain backstage in Season 1 prose; see `26-mystery-board.md` and `27-conflict-and-antagonist-board.md` for reveal discipline.

## The Observatory

Ancient organization studying timelines, consciousness, magic, reality branches, and — centrally — **Builders**: transmigrated/reincarnated consciousnesses with systems-shaping aptitude.

Core belief:

> Uncontrolled Builders can destabilize reality.

## Aion

Represents stability through control.

## Returnists

Movie 4 faction.

Belief:

> One world, one history, one truth.

Goal: collapse branches and restore the Original Timeline. They are ideologically dangerous because their argument is understandable.
