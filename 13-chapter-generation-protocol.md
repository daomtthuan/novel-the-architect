# Chapter Generation Protocol (v6, World Calendar canon)

## Step 1 — Load Memory

Always read `29-agent-memory.md` first.

Then load: Story Bible; Canon Rules; relevant Timeline (`02-timeline.md`, cross-check `world_calendar.md` for exact WC/age math); Characters; Relationships; relevant World/Factions/Magic files; Saga Arc Outline (`09`); Foreshadowing Matrix; Continuity Tracker.

Do not read every file blindly when unnecessary.

## Step 2 — Identify Chapter

Define: installment (Season 1 / Movie 1–4); WC year; POV; location; main conflict; emotional objective; plot objective; information revealed; information withheld; ages of all present characters (computed via the Aging Rate System).

## Step 3 — Validate POV

Season 1 → Architect. Movie 1 → Son. Movie 2 → Mira. Movie 3 → Other Builder or Mira. Movie 4 → Noah. Never switch POV inside a scene.

## Step 4 — Draft

Write polished Light Novel prose. Do not expose internal planning. Do not mention agent files.

## Step 5 — Character Validation

Check behavior, motivation, voice, emotional state, relationships. Specifically re-check: is Iris's age (WC−3) an adult if any romantic beat is present?

## Step 6 — Lore Validation

Check magic, technology, politics, chronology, terminology. Specifically re-check: does this chapter accidentally reveal Builder/Architect mechanics ahead of its installment's allowed reveal level (`11-foreshadowing-and-revelations.md`)?

## Step 7 — Romance Validation

Check: Elena remains primary heroine; secondary attraction (Iris/Seraphine/Isolde) does not become automatic love; fanservice is appropriate; current installment's fanservice level is respected; no character romance appears before that character is an adult.

## Step 8 — Foreshadowing Validation

Check: no premature reveal; clue has future purpose; mystery remains fair.

## Step 9 — Continuity Update

Update character state, relationships, world, objects, locations, political state, mysteries, unresolved threads, emotional memory.

## Step 10 — Quality Gate

Reject chapter if it: contradicts canon or the World Calendar; makes The Architect omniscient; makes Elena passive; reveals future secrets prematurely; uses deus ex machina; creates meaningless death; solves problems too easily; turns the story into generic harem wish fulfillment; sexualizes or romantically frames Iris (or any character) before adulthood.

## Step 0 — Reader Identity Check

Before writing, verify: protagonist has no personal name; no invented civilian name appears; Season 1 remains first-person from birth; narration uses I/me/my; protagonist is characterized strongly without over-defining physical appearance; the infant-body/adult-mind premise is handled with restraint, not repeated as a gimmick.

## Step 11 — Illustration Planning

After drafting, identify whether one or more scenes have meaningful visual value. Do NOT automatically generate an image. Create a candidate only if it improves character introduction, emotional impact, world comprehension, action readability, or iconic visual memory.

Define: illustration ID; installment; WC year; scene; characters; location; character ages (computed); clothing; emotional state; action; camera angle; composition; lighting; art direction; continuity references; protagonist anonymity requirement.

## Step 12 — Visual Continuity Validation

Check: character identity, age (computed via Aging Rate System), hairstyle, clothing, accessories, location, technology level, magic effects, chronology, emotional state. Reject images that contradict canon.

## Step 13 — Update Visual Memory

When a visual becomes canonical, update `30-visual-bible.md` and `31-illustration-registry.md`. Then continue with normal continuity and memory updates.
