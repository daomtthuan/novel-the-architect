# Saga Arc Outline: One Series + Four Movies (v6, World Calendar canon)

## Season 1 — "The Architect" (Series)

### Narrator

The Architect — first-person, from birth (WC0).

### Focus

Infant-body/adult-mind reincarnation; childhood; magic; The Teacher; his death (WC16) and the Darkest Point (WC16–23); Elena and Iris; Elena's breakthrough (WC23); marriage (WC24); Isolde's refused proposal (WC26); city-building; political factions; Demon threat; Seraphine's infiltration and defection; the Great War; The Ark; Elena's sacrifice and the Son's birth (WC35); the empty home.

### Major Acts

1. Birth and childhood (adult mind, infant body)
2. The Teacher and early magic systematization
3. Meeting Elena and Iris
4. The Teacher's death / First Major Loss
5. The Darkest Point
6. Elena's breakthrough and marriage
7. Isolde and political pressure
8. City-building and nation-building
9. Political factions
10. Demon threat and Seraphine
11. Great War
12. The Ark
13. Elena's death / Son's birth
14. Empty home

### Fanservice

Highest of the whole saga.

## Movie 1 — "The Heir"

### Narrator

The Son — first-person, framed by the Architect's death at open and close.

### Focus

First half: birth (WC35) through childhood with Iris (sword) and Seraphine (magic) to The Break (WC67, age 16). Second half: investigation, discovery of Elena's truth (WC75), reconciliation (WC79), and the Architect's death by old age (WC82) landing as the same beat as full understanding.

### Fanservice

Low; centered on found-family warmth (Iris, Seraphine) rather than romance.

## Movie 2 — "The Last Observer"

### Narrator

Mira — first-person. ~WC182, 100 years after the Architect's death.

### Focus

Historical mystery; early truth of The Ark and Elena as Quantum Anchor; first exposure to the concept of Builders.

### Fanservice

Minimal.

## Movie 3 — "Mira & The Other Builder"

### Narrators

Mira and The Other Builder — dual first-person, ~WC192.

### Ratio

Approximately 60% The Other Builder / 40% Mira.

### Focus

Full Builder/Architect lore reveal; alternate timeline; Other Builder's refusal to let his Elena choose; philosophical confrontation; optimization versus freedom.

### Rule

Never switch POV inside a scene.

## Movie 4 — "The Last Home"

### Narrator

Noah — first-person, ~WC1082, 1,000 years after the Architect's death.

### Focus

Returnists; Original Timeline; Earth connection; an ordinary alternate Builder; freedom.

### Final Scene

May briefly switch to third-person.

### Final emotional statement

The world no longer needs another Architect — or another Builder.

## No Movie 5

The story ends with Movie 4.
