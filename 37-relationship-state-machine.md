# Relationship State Machine (v6, World Calendar canon)

## Purpose

Track romantic and emotional progression so the AI agent does not randomly change relationships.

## States

UNKNOWN → ACQUAINTANCE → FRIEND → TRUSTED → CLOSE_FRIEND → ADMIRATION → ATTRACTION → ROMANTIC_TENSION → CONFESSION → COMMITTED → MARRIED → GRIEF → LEGACY_BOND

Characters may move forward or backward depending on events.

## The Architect × Elena

Expected trajectory:

UNKNOWN → ACQUAINTANCE (WC15) → FRIEND → TRUST → the WC23 ideological breakthrough → LOVE → COMMITTED → MARRIED (WC24) → FAMILY → LOSS (WC35) → LEGACY

Do not skip major emotional steps.

## The Architect × Iris

UNKNOWN → ACQUAINTANCE/FRIEND (WC15, strictly platonic — Iris is 12) → CLOSE_FRIEND (childhood/adolescence) → ADMIRATION → ATTRACTION (only once Iris is an adult, ~WC21+) → a deliberate step BACK to CLOSE_FRIEND at the wedding (WC24, her own choice) → LEGACY_BOND (post-WC35, co-parent).

Never place ATTRACTION on this track before ~WC21.

## The Architect × Seraphine

UNKNOWN → provocateur/ADVERSARY (WC33) → ROMANTIC_TENSION (one-sided, deliberately provoked) → WITNESS → ally (WC34–35) → ATTRACTION (her side, ongoing) → repeatedly offered, repeatedly held at CLOSE_FRIEND/ROMANTIC_TENSION by his refusal → LEGACY_BOND (co-parent).

Never advance this track to CONFESSION-accepted or COMMITTED.

## The Architect × Isolde

UNKNOWN → political ACQUAINTANCE (WC26) → CONFESSION (of the political proposal) → refused, held at TRUSTED ally → ATTRACTION (her side, genuine, later) → refused again → enduring TRUSTED / ally state.

## Default Secondary Heroine Trajectory (general template)

ACQUAINTANCE → FRIEND → ADMIRATION → possible ATTRACTION → acceptance / unresolved feelings / friendship.

They do not automatically become committed partners.

## State Update Rules

After any significant romantic scene, update: current state; trigger event; character interpretation; reader interpretation; unresolved feelings; next plausible development.

## Jealousy

Jealousy must reveal character.

Good: Elena notices Seraphine flirting (WC33) and later quietly asks the Architect what he actually feels — establishing trust rather than melodrama.

Bad: Elena suddenly becomes irrational because the plot needs drama.

## Confession Rule

A confession must change something. It can lead to acceptance, rejection, mutual understanding, friendship, political complications, or unresolved tension.

Applied here: Isolde's WC26 proposal IS a confession-equivalent, and its rejection reshapes the entire political subplot rather than simply closing it off.

Never use confession only as a disposable fan-service scene.
