# Dialogue Memory (v6, World Calendar canon)

These lines are thematic anchors.

## Architect

> "I don't want to become a god."

Context: he wants to build a place where people he loves can live peacefully.

> "There had to be a rule."

Context: his childhood/Darkest-Point-era default response to pain.

## Elena

> "You can't fix everything."

Use as a recurring philosophical challenge — this is the seed of the WC23 breakthrough line, "People are not variables."

## Architect to Son

> "Yes."

Response when asked whether he would make the same choice again.

Followed by:

> "Because your mother chose it."

Do not use this line casually.

## The Other Builder

> "A world that allows suffering is a failed system."

This represents his ideology — and is a colder descendant of the primary Architect's own Darkest-Point voice.

## Mira

> "History remembers results. It forgets choices."

Use as a thematic motif.

## Noah

> "Maybe the world doesn't need another Architect."

Major late-series thematic statement. May be paired, once, with: "Maybe it doesn't need another Builder, either."

## Dialogue Rule

Do not overuse signature lines. A line becomes powerful because it is rare.
