## The Architect

WC0–8: adult mind, infant body — curiosity and quiet strangeness.
WC9–15: prodigy, confidence, ambition, under The Teacher.
WC16: First Major Loss, fully dramatized (Chapter 11). (unchanged — see prior version for full detail)
WC16, immediate aftermath (Chapter 12): (unchanged — see prior version)
WC17, one year later (Chapter 13): (unchanged — see prior version)
**WC18 (Chapter 14):** takes an unauthorized hazard-B Guild mission, misreporting the threat level (three reported, eight actual), and nearly dies before self-rescuing via an improvised wide-area sealing structure — notably, he does NOT repeat the WC16 mistake of channeling Mana through his own body, showing at least partial internalization of that lesson even while repeating the underlying pattern of reckless solo risk-taking. Elena discovers both this mission and his continued solo nighttime testing of the externalized Mana-pathway concept; the WC18 major argument occurs in full, and for the first time he has no rebuttal to her critique — she directly compares him to the monster that killed his parents. Elena moves out of the shared home. Days later, when a political delegation threatens Elena and Iris to coerce him into weaponizing his research, he experiences his first on-page loss of emotional control: an undirected, overwhelming Mana outburst that frightens the delegation into retreat, followed by an explicit, cold warning. This is a new and more dangerous failure mode than his established calculation-based coping — evidence that the suppressed emotion is beginning to find uncontrolled outlets.
WC16–23: the Darkest Point — control crisis, now including both the calculation-based avoidance pattern (Chapters 12–13) and a new emotional-outburst failure mode (Chapter 14), escalating toward the WC19 reconciliation and eventually the WC22 low point.
WC23–24: breakthrough, marriage.
WC25–34: nation-builder, husband, wartime leader-in-waiting.
WC35: loss and acceptance — becomes "The Architect."
WC36–82: legacy and fatherhood.
Post-WC82: becomes historical memory (Movie 1 onward).

**Permanent physical marker (unchanged):** missing left arm from WC16 onward — see `30-visual-bible.md`, `03-characters.md`.

## Elena

WC0–14 (pre-story): independent adult of the Eterna people.
WC15–23: companion, then the one who breaks his ideology. WC16 (Chapter 11): (unchanged — see prior version) WC16, immediate aftermath (Chapter 12): (unchanged — see prior version) WC17 (Chapter 13): (unchanged — see prior version)
**WC18 (Chapter 14):** her concern escalates from direct confrontation into genuine, unguarded anger — the first time in the saga her response to the Architect is not measured patience. She discovers his unauthorized mission and continued solo pathway testing, confronts him directly, and delivers the argument's emotional climax by naming what he is becoming. When he has no answer, she moves out of the shared home — not from diminished care, but from an inability to keep watching him harm himself without being able to stop it. This is the clearest evidence yet that her love includes real boundaries, directly setting up both the WC19 reconciliation and, eventually, the WC23 breakthrough.
WC24–34: partner and co-builder, mother-to-be.
WC35: chooses sacrifice.
Post-WC35: becomes emotional and metaphysical anchor (Quantum Anchor, revealed Movie 2–3).
