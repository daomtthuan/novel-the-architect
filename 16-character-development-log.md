# Character Development Log (v6, World Calendar canon)

## The Architect

WC0–8: adult mind, infant body — curiosity and quiet strangeness.
WC9–15: prodigy, confidence, ambition, under The Teacher.
WC16–23: the Darkest Point — control crisis.
WC23–24: breakthrough, marriage.
WC25–34: nation-builder, husband, wartime leader-in-waiting.
WC35: loss and acceptance — becomes "The Architect."
WC36–82: legacy and fatherhood.
Post-WC82: becomes historical memory (Movie 1 onward).

## Elena

WC0–14 (pre-story): independent adult of the Eterna people.
WC15–23: companion, then the one who breaks his ideology.
WC24–34: partner and co-builder, mother-to-be.
WC35: chooses sacrifice.
Post-WC35: becomes emotional and metaphysical anchor (Quantum Anchor, revealed Movie 2–3).

## Iris

WC3–14: childhood, growing friendship with Elena.
WC15: introduced alongside Elena, strictly platonic (age 12).
WC21–24: adult, unspoken feelings, self-withdrawal at the marriage.
WC35 onward: co-parent, sword teacher, permanent found-family.

## Seraphine

WC33: infiltrator/seductress.
WC34: witness to the true agenda of Demon leadership.
WC35: defector.
WC36 onward: guilt-driven caretaker, gently and permanently refused, magic teacher to the Son.

## Isolde

WC26: proposes marriage, refused.
WC27–34: ally, then wartime ally.
Post-WC35: enduring, refused affection; comes to respect the refusal.

## Son

WC35: born the day his mother dies.
WC36–66: raised by his father, Iris, and Seraphine; growing ideological gap with his father.
WC67: The Break.
WC67–78: investigation.
WC79: reconciliation.
WC82: his father's death.
Post-WC82: builds his own path (Movie 1 closing arc).

## Mira

Movie 2 beginning: historian.
Middle: investigator.
Late: Observer.
Movie 3: mediator between competing realities, alongside The Other Builder.

## The Other Builder

Pre-Movie 3: certainty, grief avoided.
Middle: control.
Late: confrontation with Mira.
End: accepts imperfection — the closest he ever comes to what the primary Architect achieved at WC35.

## Noah

Movie 4 beginning: ordinary citizen.
Middle: historical investigator.
Late: defender of freedom.
End: chooses a future without destiny, and without needing to become a Builder.
