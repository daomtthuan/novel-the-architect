# Character Secrets (v7, World Calendar canon)

## The Architect

### Secret 1

He is not simply reincarnated — he is a "Builder," one of many transmigrated consciousnesses across history and timelines.
**Known by:** protagonist, partially, from early on (he suspects something is unusual about himself well before he has words for it).
**Reveal to reader as explicit lore:** Movie 2.

### Secret 2

He blames himself, privately and permanently, for The Teacher's death — and the Darkest Point is the direct result.
**Known by:** protagonist fully; Elena partially (WC23 onward, though she begins to sense something is wrong as early as WC16, Chapter 12); the Son eventually (WC75–79).
**Reveal:** Season 1 (reader knows in real time); Movie 1 (Son learns).

### Secret 3

He creates systems whose capabilities exceed his original understanding (e.g., the Ark's later stages).
**Reveal:** gradually, Movie 2–3.

## Elena

### Secret 1

She has recurring resonance with other timelines (her Quantum Anchor nature).
**Reader knowledge:** subtle clues in Season 1.
**Full reveal:** Movie 2–3.

### Secret 2

Her choice to become The Ark's core is fully voluntary.
**Reveal:** Season 1 climax (WC35).

## Iris

### Secret

She has loved the Architect since she was old enough for it to matter, and never once said so. She only expresses it indirectly—through cooking for him, caring for him, offering advice, and checking in on him—especially during the Architect in Darkest time. Her caretaking pattern is already visible in its entirely age-appropriate, platonic form as early as WC15–16 (Chapters 8, 11, 12) — mending, sharing food, practical errands, and, at WC16, a rare direct admission that she is deferring her own grief until she is "no longer needed."
**Reveal:** implied throughout Season 1; stated outright, if ever, only much later and gently (e.g., a single Movie 1 scene where the Son realizes it on his own).

## Seraphine

### Secret 1

Her original infiltration mission (WC33) is the indirect reason Elena grew afraid enough to accelerate certain wartime decisions — Seraphine privately believes she bears some responsibility for the timeline that led to Elena's sacrifice.
**Reveal:** hinted early; stated directly only once, post-WC35, in a vulnerable scene with the Architect.

### Secret 2

She is genuinely in love with the Architect, and has permanently lost her demonic combat power because of it (a Succubus who truly falls in love loses that power). She never tells him the power-loss is connected to her feelings for him — she lets him assume it is simply a consequence of defection or war fatigue.
**Reveal:** to the reader, gradually across her post-WC35 scenes; to the Son, much later, possibly only in Movie 1; the Architect may never learn the true cause at all — agent's discretion, but do not reveal it to him casually.

## Isolde

### Secret

Her genuine feelings developed only after the political motive was already refused — she is privately relieved, not offended, that the Architect never accepted her for the "wrong" reason.
**Reveal:** Season 1, mid-to-late.

## Son

### Secret

He initially considers rejecting his father's entire legacy.
**Reveal:** Movie 1.

## Mira

### Secret

She begins investigating the Architect partly because she distrusts the official historical record.
**Reveal:** early Movie 2.

## The Other Builder

### Secret

He is not a random alternate villain. He is a genuine version of the same person who, at the same choice, chose control over his Elena's freedom — and never became an Architect because of it.
**Reveal:** Movie 3.

## Noah

### Secret

He has no predetermined cosmic destiny, and no Builder aptitude at all. This becomes thematically important.
**Reveal:** progressively in Movie 4.

## The Teacher (deceased, WC16)

### Secret

He had private, personal reasons for refusing to take on new students for years before accepting the Architect (WC9) — reasons connected to "someone who thought this way" (Chapter 4's unfinished remark) and possibly to his sharp, unexplained attentiveness toward Elena (Chapter 7–8).
**Known by:** The Teacher himself (deceased, WC16); confirmed, but not detailed, to be known by the Guild Master (Chapter 12 — "that is not my story to tell").
**Reveal:** UNRESOLVED. Permanently unresolvable from The Teacher's own mouth following his death. Whether the Guild Master ever reveals what he knows is entirely open and must not be decided without explicit owner direction. Do not have another character casually explain this away.
