# Romance Foreshadowing Map (v6, World Calendar canon)

## Purpose

Romantic interactions should sometimes plant future emotional consequences.

## Elena

Early: shared dream of a home; small domestic habits; private jokes; understanding each other's silence; her patience through his Darkest Point (WC16–23).

Middle: discussions about family; disagreement about sacrifice; conversations about what a peaceful world means; her quiet resonance/dreams (later revealed as Quantum Anchor activity).

Late: subtle preparations; unfinished conversations; objects that later become emotional anchors.

After death: other characters (Iris, Seraphine, the Son, Mira) remember her differently; her choices become evidence; her relationship with the Architect becomes part of history and, eventually, multiverse lore.

## Iris

Foreshadow (from ~WC15, strictly platonic at that stage): quiet loyalty; noticing his habits before she has any reason to; defending his ideas without yet understanding why it matters to her. Any romantic-coded foreshadowing must wait until she is textually an adult (~WC21+).

## Seraphine

Foreshadow: her early teasing hides real perceptiveness; she is one of the first to see through the Architect's public composure, even while trying to seduce him for entirely different reasons; her eventual guilt is seeded in how _easily_ she succeeds at provoking Elena's jealousy (WC33) — planting the idea that her actions have real consequences.

## Isolde

Foreshadow: political conversations become personal; she begins distinguishing the man from the political asset well before WC26; her relief-not-offense at being refused is foreshadowed by how she frames the original proposal (practical, not romantic, in her own words).

## Rule

Foreshadowing must never imply that every secondary heroine will become a wife. The purpose is to make relationships feel alive.
