## Mystery: Who / What Was the WC16 Demon? (new, v8)
Status: unknown Season 1 (appears only as "a high-tier Demon"); Hinted Season 1 via indirect clues only (an old record, a line in the Scout's notes, a detail Seraphine can't explain about her own faction's leadership); connective tissue to WC33–35 not to be spelled out before Movie 1 at the earliest.
Clues: Scout's WC16 discovery of "unusual Demon activity"; the Demon's unnatural strength and tactical patience during the WC16 battle; the domination-seeking Demon faction's otherwise-unexplained confidence escalating toward WC33–35.
False interpretation: the WC16 Demon was simply an unusually strong, isolated threat that died in the battle.
Partial truth: it survived, badly wounded.
True answer: it is an ancient demon lord, believed sealed/destroyed generations before WC0 ("the Sealing" — left as ambient deep-history, not further explained unless the owner wants to expand it), referred to in continuity notes as **"The Unburied One."** True name intentionally never given. Critically wounded at WC16, it spends WC16–32 dormant while quietly influencing the domination-seeking Demon faction from the shadows — a hidden patron, not a commander; Seraphine's faction leadership remains self-directed and unaware of the connection, and Seraphine herself never learns of it. Presumed (not confirmed) destroyed when Demon territories collapse at WC40.
Reveal chapter: not yet blocked out — no earlier than Movie 1.
Emotional consequence: if ever revealed to the Architect or the Son, it would recontextualize WC16 as never fully "solved" — the very failure that broke him quietly helped shape the war that later killed Elena, without him ever knowing. Handle with care; this should deepen tragedy, not manufacture a cheap twist.
