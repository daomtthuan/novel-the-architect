# Character Voice Guide (v6, World Calendar canon)

## The Architect

### Speech
concise · analytical · dry humor · occasionally blunt · increasingly warm with Elena · noticeably colder during the Darkest Point (WC16–23)

### Internal Voice
First-person from birth, technical metaphors used naturally, occasionally self-aware about the strangeness of an adult mind narrating a child's actions in early chapters (used sparingly).

Early childhood: > "There had to be a rule. There was always a rule."

The Teacher's death / Darkest Point: > "If I had modeled one more variable, he would still be alive."

After Elena breaks through: > "Not everything needed a rule."

After Elena's death: shorter, quieter, less explanatory.

## Elena

### Speech
warm · playful · direct · emotionally perceptive · capable of cutting through his logic

She may tease him when he becomes too serious, and is the one person who can name his Darkest Point out loud without flinching.

### Signature Pattern
She often asks a simple emotional question after he gives a complicated technical answer.

## Iris

### Speech
plainspoken · loyal · dry rather than dramatic · says less than she feels

She rarely states her own feelings directly, even to herself; her voice should carry restraint as a personality trait, not just as plot convenience.

## Seraphine

### Speech
teasing · confident · deliberately provocative early on, then increasingly sincere after her defection · uses humor to deflect from guilt

Her lines should get warmer and less performative as the story goes on, without losing her playfulness entirely.

## Isolde

### Speech
composed · diplomatic · precise · occasionally lets real feeling slip through practiced language

## Son

### Speech
more emotional than his father · occasionally sarcastic · defensive during adolescence · calmer with maturity

His internal narration should notice human details his father overlooked, and should distinguish Iris's and Seraphine's very different influences on how he talks.

## Mira

### Speech
precise · skeptical · curious · occasionally dry

Her narration frequently compares evidence. Avoid making her sound like an exposition machine.

## The Other Builder

### Speech
calm · controlled · precise · frighteningly reasonable

He rarely raises his voice. His horror comes from how logical he sounds — and from how close his cadence is to the primary Architect's Darkest Point voice.

## Noah

### Speech
ordinary · conversational · observant · less grandiose

He should sound unlike the legendary Architect, and should never use "Builder" language naturally — it's foreign to him.

## Voice Separation Rule

If a paragraph's narrator could be swapped between characters without changing its feeling, the voice is too generic.
