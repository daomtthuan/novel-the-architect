## Protagonist — The Architect

### Identity

No personal name. "The Architect" is a title, earned at WC35, never a birth name. Distinct from the broader category "Builder."

### Visual Principle

Reader-projectable in Season 1. He is born WC0 and physically ages as a normal Human (1.0×) despite an adult mind from birth — so early-childhood illustrations should show a genuinely young child's body with unusually still, focused eyes/expression as the only visual hint of the adult mind within.

### Canonical Physical Reference

Height: 178 cm (adult, corrected canon — same at all adult life stages).
Build: lean / agile.
Hair: black, medium-length, slightly tousled.
Eyes: dark grey-brown, calm/analytical by default.
Element affinity: Quantum / Space.
Signature tool: Blueprint Codex, Arcanic Pen, Quantum Compass.
Core palette: black, white, gold, deep blue, with pale magic accents.

### PERMANENT INJURY (NEW, v7 — hard canon from WC16 onward)

**Missing left arm.** At WC16 (First Major Loss, Chapter 11), the Architect's left arm is destroyed as backlash from channeling raw, uncontrolled Mana through his own body to power a self-designed high-tier spell. From WC16 onward, in **every** illustration, design reference, and physical description — childhood-era (pre-WC16) illustrations are unaffected, but **all WC16-and-later depictions must show the missing left arm** unless the owner explicitly authorizes a prosthetic or magical replacement in a future canon decision. Do not silently restore the arm. Do not silently add a prosthetic without explicit instruction. When composition allows, prefer angles (three-quarter, over-the-shoulder) that can either display or tastefully imply the injury without requiring graphic medical detail, consistent with the project's existing preference for restrained protagonist framing.

### Preferred Framing

back view · three-quarter back · side silhouette · over-the-shoulder · partially obscured face

### Avoid

distinctive face as default S1 choice · excessive facial close-ups in Season 1 · invented name in any caption/prompt · fixed celebrity likeness · unnecessary physical measurements in prose · silently omitting or restoring the missing left arm in post-WC16 depictions

### Visual Evolution (updated eras)

- **Childhood (WC0–14):** small, plain, practical clothing; visually an ordinary child; The Teacher's training gear appears from WC10. Both arms present.
- **Adolescence pre-WC16 (WC15):** growing into the canonical build. Both arms present.
- **Post-WC16 (WC16 onward):** **missing left arm**, permanently. Early post-tragedy clothing/bandaging may reflect recent amputation trauma; later stages should show the injury as a settled, integrated part of his silhouette rather than a fresh wound, unless a specific scene calls for the earlier stage.
- **Elena era (WC17–23):** growing into the canonical build, now with missing left arm; clothing darkens/simplifies during the Darkest Point.
- **Marriage era (WC24+):** ceremonial black-and-white layered coat, gold star/compass filigree, deep blue cloak lining; Architect's Ring, Eterna Bond, Quantum Compass — clothing/design should account for the missing left arm (e.g., asymmetric tailoring, a pinned or draped left sleeve) rather than ignoring it.
- **Great War (WC32–35):** functional combat/command clothing, stripped of ceremony; missing left arm remains.
- **Post-War / Fatherhood (WC36–66):** mature, simpler clothing; missing left arm remains.
- **Final years (WC67–82):** hair fully white/silver; build "lean/frail but statuesque"; carries the Blueprint Codex, Architect's Staff, Elena's Pendant, Memory Hourglass, Star Map Scrolls — missing left arm remains, a lifelong visual marker of WC16.
