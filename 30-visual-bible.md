## Protagonist — The Architect

### Identity
No personal name. "The Architect" is a title, earned at WC35, never a birth name. Distinct from the broader category "Builder."

### Visual Principle
Reader-projectable in Season 1. He is born WC0 and physically ages as a normal Human (1.0×) despite an adult mind from birth — so early-childhood illustrations should show a genuinely young child's body with unusually still, focused eyes/expression as the only visual hint of the adult mind within.

### Canonical Physical Reference
Height: 178 cm (adult, corrected canon — same at all adult life stages).
Build: lean / agile.
Hair: black, medium-length, slightly tousled.
Eyes: dark grey-brown, calm/analytical by default.
Element affinity: Quantum / Space.
Signature tool: Blueprint Codex, Arcanic Pen, Quantum Compass.
Core palette: black, white, gold, deep blue, with pale magic accents.

### Preferred Framing
back view · three-quarter back · side silhouette · over-the-shoulder · partially obscured face

### Avoid
distinctive face as default S1 choice · excessive facial close-ups in Season 1 · invented name in any caption/prompt · fixed celebrity likeness · unnecessary physical measurements in prose

### Visual Evolution (updated eras)
- **Childhood (WC0–14):** small, plain, practical clothing; visually an ordinary child; The Teacher's training gear appears from WC10.
- **Adolescence / Elena era (WC15–23):** growing into the canonical build; clothing darkens/simplifies during the Darkest Point.
- **Marriage era (WC24+):** ceremonial black-and-white layered coat, gold star/compass filigree, deep blue cloak lining; Architect's Ring, Eterna Bond, Quantum Compass.
- **Great War (WC32–35):** functional combat/command clothing, stripped of ceremony.
- **Post-War / Fatherhood (WC36–66):** mature, simpler clothing.
- **Final years (WC67–82):** hair fully white/silver — the only element that legitimately changes with age; build "lean/frail but statuesque"; carries the Blueprint Codex, Architect's Staff, Elena's Pendant, Memory Hourglass, Star Map Scrolls.

## Seraphine

### Race
Succubus (Demon). Ageless — appearance never changes across the entire saga.

### Nickname
"Sera" — used by the Architect after her post-WC35 visits begin, at her own request, as acknowledgment that she is no longer his enemy. Not a term of romantic reciprocation.

### Canonical Reference (NEW)
Build: striking, confident posture. Hair: deep red or violet, worn loose. Eyes: catlike, amber or crimson. Small demonic markers (e.g., subtle horns, a tail) present but stylized — tasteful, not grotesque, not overly sexualized per the project's fanservice-tone rules.
Core palette: dark reds, black, gold accents — visually the most dramatic of the four heroines.
Signature tool: none required pre-WC35; her "tool" is her own presence/charm early on, later replaced narratively by genuine magical teaching ability (as the Son's tutor).

### Power-Loss Continuity Note
Once she genuinely falls in love (sometime post-WC35), a Succubus permanently loses demonic combat power and retains only ordinary magic. Illustrations of Seraphine from this point onward should drop any battle-ready demonic effects/markers used in her WC33–35 infiltrator-era designs, replacing them with a plainer magic-caster visual register consistent with her new role teaching the Son.

## Art Direction

Default: cinematic anime / high-quality light novel illustration. Maintain consistent proportions, coherent lighting, readable silhouettes, cinematic composition, restrained visual effects.

The "Architect's Line" gold star/compass filigree motif (Architect, Elena, Son, and by extension Mira through affiliation) remains canonical family/legacy shorthand. Iris, Seraphine, Isolde, and The Teacher deliberately do NOT share this motif — visually marking them as beloved but outside the direct lineage.

Do not let image generation turn every scene into an overproduced action poster.
