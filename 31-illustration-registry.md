## Rules

1. Never assign the protagonist a personal name.
2. Keep recurring characters visually consistent.
3. Record major costume changes.
4. Record age progression (via computed formula, not raw WC).
5. Record iconic locations.
6. Record artifacts such as The Ark and the Eterna Ring/Bond.
7. If an image contradicts prose canon, prose canon wins and the image is rejected.
8. If an image establishes a new visual fact, update the Visual Bible before reusing it.
9. Never generate a romantically-framed illustration of Iris set before ~WC21.

## Fanservice Illustration Rule

Fanservice illustrations are allowed within the project's light-fanservice tone. They must remain tasteful and consistent with character age and story context — Iris only once adult; Seraphine tasteful despite her Succubus design; Isolde consistent with her formal-then-genuine arc. They should never override the central narrative or turn the work into explicit sexual content.
