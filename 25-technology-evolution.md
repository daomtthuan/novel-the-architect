# Technology Evolution (v6, World Calendar canon)

## Stage 0 — Native Magic

Personal spellcasting.

## Stage 1 — Standardization

Magic terminology and reproducible spells, begun under The Teacher (WC10–16).

## Stage 2 — Infrastructure

Mana grids; magical lighting; communication; sanitation; logistics.

## Stage 3 — Industrial Magic

Automated systems; mass production; transportation; teleportation.

## Stage 4 — Strategic Magic

Defensive networks; battlefield systems; the Ark (completed by WC35).

## Stage 4.5 — The Reform (~WC48–52)

The Architect deliberately dismantles or rebuilds several Darkest-Point-era systems that risked treating people as variables. Unique to this canon — a technological "undoing" era with strong thematic weight.

## Stage 5 — Post-War Integration

Magic and engineering become integrated civilization infrastructure.

## Stage 6 — Observatory Science

Space-time observation; formal study of Builders (Movie 2).

## Stage 7 — Reality Engineering

Timeline interaction (Movie 3).

This should feel progressively more dangerous and abstract.

## Rule

Technology must have social consequences. Every major technological leap should affect jobs, class, military balance, trade, politics, culture.
