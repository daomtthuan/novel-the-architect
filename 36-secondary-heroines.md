# Secondary Heroines (v6, World Calendar canon)

All romantic/fan-service framing in this file applies only once each character is an adult. Iris specifically is introduced as a child (age 12, WC15) and must be written as strictly platonic at that stage.

---

## 1. Iris

### Narrative Function

Elena's best friend from before the story begins; later the Son's sword teacher and co-parent.

### Race / Aging

Human, 1.0× rate, born WC3 (age = WC−3).

### Personality

- direct;
- loyal;
- physically capable;
- quietly self-restrained;
- dryly funny rather than dramatic.

### Relationship

Introduced WC15 as a close platonic friend of both Elena and the Architect (age 12 — strictly no romantic framing). Her own feelings develop only once she is an adult, roughly WC21–24. She never confesses. When the Architect and Elena marry (WC24), she consciously steps back and chooses to remain close to both as trusted family.

### Main Story Function

After Elena's death (WC35), she becomes one of the Son's two primary caretakers, and his sword instructor — playful training from ~WC42, formal First Sword Training ~WC43.

### Romance Function

Quiet, unspoken, permanently unresolved. She is the emotional throughline proving that not every attraction needs a confession scene to matter.

### Fan-Service Function

Minimal and restrained by design — training scenes, practical banter, occasional teasing from Seraphine about her restraint. Never generate romantic/fan-service illustrations of her set before ~WC21.

## 2. Seraphine

### Narrative Function

Originally a Succubus sent by Demon leadership to investigate and destabilize the Architect; later a defector, guilt-driven caretaker, and the Son's magic teacher.

### Race / Aging

Succubus (Demon). Ageless — appearance never changes.

### Personality

- playful;
- perceptive;
- confident;
- mischievous;
- unusually honest about attraction;
- privately guilt-ridden after WC35.

### Relationship

Arrives WC33 and deliberately seduces/provokes the Architect, triggering Elena's jealousy. By WC34 she recognizes the Demon leadership's true domination-seeking agenda; defects to the Architect's side at WC35, the moment of Elena's sacrifice. After Elena's death she visits frequently, offering physical and emotional comfort — the Architect always refuses; he remains devoted to Elena.

She eventually asks him to call her **"Sera"** as acknowledgment that she is no longer his enemy — he agrees to the name, not to anything more. She is privately in love with him; he never reciprocates. Once a Succubus truly falls in love, she permanently loses her demonic combat power and can use only ordinary magic — this happens to Seraphine after WC35, and is the actual reason she stops fighting and settles into teaching the Son magic from ~WC48 onward.

### Main Story Function

Humanizes Demon society; complicates the human/demon conflict; provides the clearest "guilt seeking absolution" arc among the secondary heroines.

### Romance Function

Permanently offered, permanently and gently refused — this refusal is one of the Architect's clearest characterization beats after Elena's death.

### Fan-Service Function

Mostly verbal teasing, playful proximity, comedic tension pre-WC35; warmer but still non-graphic scenes post-WC35. Do not turn her into a purely sexual character, and never depict her "comfort" scenes as fulfilled.

## 3. Isolde

### Narrative Function

Political bridge between the Architect's growing power and the established Kingdoms.

### Personality

- composed;
- strategic;
- educated;
- politically aware;
- privately playful;
- ultimately relieved rather than offended by his refusals.

### Relationship

Proposes a political marriage at WC26; refused. Chooses to remain an ally anyway. Genuine feelings develop over the following years. Refused again even once Elena herself consents to a second marriage — establishing that his refusal is his own principle, not merely deference to his wife.

### Main Story Function

Connects romance to diplomacy, treaties, trade, and royal politics; becomes a critical wartime ally from WC32.

### Romance Conflict

Her feelings create political complications because affection can be read as a state alliance. The Architect must avoid exploiting her feelings for political gain — and never does.

## Removed / Replaced Roles

The previous placeholder roster (Elven Mage, Beastkin Warrior, Demon Woman, Adult Scholar Girl) is **retired** in favor of the three named heroines above, who now carry all secondary romance and fan-service functions. If the project owner wants any of the retired archetypes reintroduced as distinct characters alongside Iris/Seraphine/Isolde, that is a new addition requiring explicit confirmation — do not silently reinstate them.
